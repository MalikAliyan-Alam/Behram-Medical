What Are Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners are custom-made, transparent, removable trays designed to gradually move your teeth into the correct position. They're a modern alternative to traditional metal braces — nearly invisible, comfortable to wear, and removable whenever you need to eat, drink, or clean your teeth.

Aligners are well-suited for correcting a wide range of orthodontic concerns, including crooked teeth, gaps between teeth, mild crowding, and many bite issues. Treatment is planned in detail from the start, so you'll have a clear sense of how your smile will progress at each stage.

For some cases, traditional braces may still be the better option — particularly for more complex bite or alignment issues. The right approach is determined during your consultation, where we look at your goals, your teeth, and what will give you the best long-term result.

The Aligner Treatment Process

Aligner treatment is carefully planned from your first visit so you know exactly what to expect at each stage.

  1. 1

    Consultation & Scan

    A thorough examination and digital impressions or scans of your teeth to plan your treatment with precision.

  2. 2

    Custom Treatment Plan

    A step-by-step plan is created, showing how your teeth will gradually move into alignment over the course of treatment.

  3. 3

    Wearing Your Aligners

    You wear a series of custom aligners, switching to the next set every couple of weeks as your teeth gently shift into position.

  4. 4

    Achieving Your New Smile

    Once treatment is complete, a retainer helps maintain your beautifully aligned smile for the long term.

What to Expect During Treatment

For the best results, aligners should be worn 20–22 hours a day, removing them only to eat, drink, and clean your teeth. When you switch to a new set of trays, it's normal to feel mild pressure or tightness for a day or two — that's a sign the aligners are doing their job and gently moving your teeth.

Regular check-ins help us monitor your progress and make sure everything is on track. Between visits, our team is available on WhatsApp for any questions or concerns you have throughout your treatment journey — so you're never on your own as your smile transforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are clear aligners painful?

Aligners are generally comfortable to wear. You may feel mild pressure or tightness when switching to a new set — this is normal and means your teeth are moving as planned.

How long does aligner treatment take?

Treatment time varies depending on your case, typically ranging from several months to over a year. We'll give you a clearer estimate after your consultation.

Can adults use clear aligners?

Absolutely. Clear aligners are popular with adults precisely because they're discreet and don't interfere with daily life, work, or appearance.

How often do I wear them?

For the best results, aligners should be worn 20–22 hours a day, removing them only to eat, drink, and clean your teeth.
